Lunging Sword

Every swipe
is a lunge.

A dungeon puzzle game for iPhone. Your sword slides until a stone, a wall or a monster stops it. Clear the monsters, gather the coins and reach the gate.

In the dungeon

  • 36 levels

    Six tiers, each bringing in one idea before the next mixes it in: movement, coins, then every monster in turn. Every level can be solved, and each awards up to three stars.

  • 4 monsters

    Regulars fall in one hit. Stonebound turn to stone and reshape the board. Armored take two hits. Mages strike down a straight line.

  • Where you stop

    Sliding past a monster is safe. Ending your move beside one, or in a mage’s line of fire, ends the run. There is no undo, so every swipe counts.
