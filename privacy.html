LUNGING SWORD

Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE]

Lunging Sword collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never connects to the internet. Everything the game remembers stays on your iPhone.

01What the game keeps on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the game stores a few things in its own private storage on your iPhone:

Level progress
Which levels you have unlocked, and the best star count you have earned on each.
Tutorials seen
Which how-to-play cards you have already read, so they are not shown again.
Settings
Your Vibration and Sound effects switches.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and there is no copy of it anywhere except on your device.

02What the game never does

  • No account, sign-in or email address.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No third-party code or SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No network connections of any kind.

03Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the game's progress in that backup, just as it does for most apps. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy and your backup settings; the game itself sends nothing anywhere.

04Deleting your data

  • Reset your progress in the game under Settings → Reset progress. This erases your stars, unlocked levels and seen tutorials. Your settings are kept.
  • Erase everything by deleting the app. That removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

05Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

06Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes what it stores or starts sending any data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
